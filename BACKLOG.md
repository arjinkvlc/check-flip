## Next release

- **Bots use their cards (reported 2026-10-03):** bots sometimes pay big checks, or are even knocked out, while holding a card that would have helped. Causes found in `public/js/engine.js`:
  - `botRandom` (the "careless" move easy/normal bots make part of the time) never plays a card at dinner, it always just pays.
  - `botBest` uses cards only above fixed thresholds (e.g. Tighten the Belt only if its own share is ≥ ¤15, Pass the Check only if the check is more than half its money) and has no "last resort" rule: when the check is more than it can pay, it should first play any card that lowers or moves the check (Pass the Check, Dutch, coupon, belt) or try a deal.
  - Fix: a last-resort step that runs for every bot level (never get knocked out while holding a helpful card), careless moves still allowed elsewhere; bot-vs-bot simulation in tests/simulate.mjs to check card use and that easy/normal/hard still differ.
- **Shortcut and Go back squares move 1–4 (decided 2026-10-03):** today Kısayol / Shortcut always moves 3 squares forward and Geri Git / Go back 3 squares back (`engine.js`, cases 'kisa' and 'geri'). Both become a random 1–4, shown in the log and on the info card ("Shortcut: 2 squares forward", "Go back: 4 squares"); update both squares' descriptions in all six languages ("1–4 squares forward / back"), the rules text and the bot's square scores.
- **Dinner venue draw animation at the start of the day (reported 2026-10-03):** since v1.10 tonight's venue is drawn when the day starts (shown under the dice), but the spinning "picking the venue" animation still plays at the end of the day (`venueReveal` in app.js, called when dinner starts), when everyone already knows the venue. Move the spinning draw to the day start (when the venue is actually picked, with the "Tonight's dinner" text appearing after it); at dinner time just show the venue card (owner, commission, who pays) without spinning.
- **Offers below the venue's price (asked 2026-10-03):** today an offer for someone's venue must be at least its last price + ¤10. Keep that as the pre-filled amount, but allow any amount from ¤5 up (an owner short of money may want to sell cheap); the owner still accepts or refuses. Bots: accept a low offer when they need the money (can't cover the coming check), otherwise as today. Texts in six languages if the hint under the box changes.
- **Tighten the Belt taken from the board square is not secret (asked 2026-10-03):** everyone saw the player land on the Tighten the Belt square, so that card stays visible in the player's hand for the others (no "?" / hidden card). Cards drawn from the Chance / Event decks stay hidden as today.
- Economy check 2026-10-03 (no change decided): simulated 600–1500 bot games per setup. In 3–4 player games buying venues clearly pays (buyers win 35% / 31% vs fair 33% / 25%; non-buyers 21% / 5%). In 2 player games venues are about neutral (buyers win ~49%) and lowering the price to ¤20–30 or raising rent/collect ×1.5–2 barely changes that. So a cheaper venue alone wouldn't fix 2-player balance; revisit with real player data.
- **Screens "reload" by themselves a few seconds after opening (reported 2026-10-04):** not a real page reload. Likely cause: account updates arriving in the background (first friends-list poll ~5 s after load, profile refresh, the v1.16 language save, token refresh) each call render(), which rebuilds the whole screen's HTML (home, leaderboard, admin, profile) even when nothing changed: animations replay, scroll position and open boxes reset, so it looks like a restart. Fix: only replace a screen's HTML when it actually changed (keep scroll and open details), and don't redraw the leaderboard/admin screens for account events that don't concern them. Verify with a test that counts DOM rebuilds over 15 s on each screen while logged in.

## Seasonal themes (decided 2026-10-04)

- **Events and dates (updated 2026-10-04, about a month each):** Halloween 15 Oct – 15 Nov; New Year 1 Dec – 31 Jan; Valentine's Day 1 – 28/29 Feb; Easter from 3 weeks before to 1 week after Western Easter Sunday (e.g. 7 Mar – 4 Apr 2027). Never two at once: if Easter's window overlaps another event, the other event wins on those days (Easter shows only on its free days). Later: an event-only game mode per event.
- **During an event:** themed decorations on the home screen and the board (pumpkins/cobwebs, snow/garlands, hearts, eggs), theme colours, and a themed variation of the menu and game music that stays recognisable as the usual tune (Halloween: minor, slower, organ and bells; New Year: sleigh bells, celesta).
- **Limited cosmetics:** for each event an avatar (e.g. pumpkin-head, Santa, Easter bunny, a Valentine one), a board style, a dice skin and a chat bubble pattern, plus event achievements and event quests that can ONLY be completed during that event's dates (server-side check of the date, like the seasons). Once earned they stay in the account forever.
- **Settings:** "Seasonal themes" switch (on by default) turns off the decorations, colours and music; event quests/achievements stay available.
- All texts in six languages; all art drawn in the same style as the board icons / avatars (no copyrighted characters).
- **Halloween goes into the next release** so it is live before 15 Oct.
- **Decorations (approved 2026-10-04):** only in a fixed page-background layer behind the whole app (the grey/white area), never on the board or cards; every event uses the same layer. Halloween: cobwebs in three corners, two swaying spiders, flying bats, blinking yellow eyes near the edges, moon, bottom silhouette (hills, graves, dead trees, fence), purple page gradient. Rich in dark mode, subtle in light mode; no animation with reduced motion. Button colours stay red.
- **Halloween quests (approved, "hard"):** finish 8 games → pumpkin dice; win 5 games → pumpkin-head avatar; make 5 deals → spooky chat bubble; finish 25 games → haunted board; all four → "Halloween 2026" achievement and title. Games against bots count for "finish games" but not for wins (wins only online/quick).
- **New Year, Valentine's Day, Easter (approved 2026-10-04, in v1.17 so they switch on by themselves):** backgrounds, items and music as previewed (New Year: string lights, pine sprig, snow, snowy village, Santa / frost dice / snowy forest / gift wrap; Valentine's: heart garland, rose vines, floating hearts, Cupid / rose dice / candlelight / love letter; Easter: blossom branches, petals, butterflies, eggs and bunny, Easter Bunny / painted egg dice / flower meadow / pastel bubble). Event-specific quests: New Year 8 games, 10 checks paid, 5 wins, 25 games; Valentine's 8 games, 5 deals, 5 wins, 25 games; Easter 8 games, 15 cards, 5 restaurants bought, 5 wins. Titles "New Year 2027", "Valentine's Day 2027", "Easter 2027"; every year's run gets its own title.
- **Event dates for players:** every event cosmetic and achievement shows "Can be earned 15 Oct – 15 Nov" on hover (Easter: "during the Easter period"); an event card on the home screen (quests, progress, end date) and an "Event" tab in the profile.
- **Admin preview of themes (asked 2026-10-04):** in the admin screen a "Theme preview" switch: Off / Halloween / New Year / Valentine's / Easter. It changes only the admin's own browser (decorations, colours, music, themed cosmetics shown in Profile & looks as a preview), whatever the date; nobody else sees it and it never unlocks or awards event items, quests or achievements (those stay date-checked on the server). Remembered in this browser until switched off.
