A free online board game to play with friends

Check Flip is a board game you play in the browser with 2–6 friends: one person makes a room, sends the code or link, and everyone joins from a phone or computer. No download, no sign-up.

Create a room and send the link

How to play with friends in one minute

  1. Open checkflipgame.com and tap With friends → Create a room.
  2. Send the 5-letter room code or the invite link on WhatsApp, Discord or anywhere.
  3. Friends open the link, pick a nickname and an avatar. The host presses Start.

Why it works well for a group

  • Everyone can join: it runs in any modern browser, on Android, iPhone, Windows or Mac.
  • Short and loud: a Quick game takes about 15 minutes; every evening someone has to pay the whole table’s dinner check, so there is always something to argue about.
  • Deals and bluffs: the payer may offer money to pass the check on, restaurants can be bought from their owners, and cards like Tighten the Belt or Discount coupon change the bill.
  • Voice-call friendly: turns are clear and quick, which makes it a good game next to a Discord or video call.
  • Fair when someone drops: a bot plays the turns of a player who loses connection until they are back.

Game modes

  • Classic: the last player at the table wins.
  • Quick: 10 days, the richest player wins.
  • 2 vs 2: teams of two; if you can’t pay, your teammate covers the difference.
  • No friends online right now? Play against bots (easy, normal or hard) or join a public table with Quick play.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Check Flip is completely free and has no pay-to-win items.

Do my friends need an account?

No. Anyone can join a room with just a nickname. A free account adds XP, levels, cosmetics, friends and the monthly season leaderboard.

How many players can play?

Rooms with friends take 2 to 6 players. You can also add bots to fill empty seats.

Can we play on phones?

Yes. The game is made for phones as much as for computers, and phone and computer players can share a table.
