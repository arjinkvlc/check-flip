      What is Check Flip?

      Check Flip is a free multiplayer board game you play in the browser, on a phone or a computer, with no download and no sign-up needed. 2 to 6 players move around a board full of restaurants, roll the dice, draw Chance and Event cards and try to stay hungry, because at the end of every day one player picks up the check for the whole table. Buy restaurants, earn commission, make deals to pass the check on, and be the last one at the table.

      Play online with friends using a room code, join a quick game with other players, play against bots (easy, normal or hard) or take turns on one device. Classic, Quick (10 days) and 2 vs 2 team modes. With a free account you earn XP, unlock avatars, dice and boards, climb the monthly season leaderboard and win medals. In English, Turkish, Spanish, Portuguese, French and German.

      How to play Check Flip online

      To play with friends, tap With friends, create a room and share the room code or the invite link in your group chat. Everyone joins from their own phone or computer, and the host starts the game. Nobody free right now? Quick play seats you at a public table, and bots fill any empty chairs.

      On your turn, roll two dice and move the total or just one die, so you can aim for the square you want. Doubles roll again. Everyone gets a little hungrier every morning, and each lap around the board adds more hunger and raises your multiplier.

      When the day ends, the next player in line pays the dinner check for the whole table, their own meal included, and the check grows with everyone’s hunger. The payer gets one offer to wriggle out of it (“I’ll give you this much, you pay the check”) or can play a card like Pass the check or Tighten the Belt.

      Restaurants are the long game: buy one, collect a fee when others land on it, cash the register or upgrade it, and take a cut of the check whenever dinner is held there. Whoever can’t pay leaves the table, and the last one still sitting wins.
